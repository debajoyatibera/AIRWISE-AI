# AIRWISE AI — Live Demo Script

**Target Duration:** ~3 minutes

## 1. Opening & Dashboard Walkthrough (45 seconds)
1. Open the app on the **Dashboard** tab. Point out that **Kolkata** loads automatically as the default city with live modelled data from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API.
2. Highlight the **Modelled Atmospheric Estimate** attribution banner—emphasizing that AIRWISE accurately labels Open-Meteo estimates as modelled data rather than claiming physical CPCB station readings.
3. Show the **US AQI** card, individual pollutant breakdowns (**PM2.5**, **PM10**, **Ozone**, **NO₂**), the **Cleanest Forecast Windows** strip, and toggle the Recharts visualization between **24h Trend** and **Hourly Forecast**.
4. Switch quickly to **Delhi** (or search a city like **Pune**) and click **Refresh** to demonstrate live geocoding and air-quality retrieval.

## 2. Personalized GenAI Daily Planner (45 seconds)
1. Navigate to **Daily AI Planner**.
2. Select a sensitive profile (e.g., **Respiratory-sensitive user** or **Child or family**), enter a work/school schedule and commute preference, and click **Generate Personalized Daily Plan**.
3. Highlight the transparent mode banner (showing either **Google Gemini** or **Rule-Based Fallback**) and point out how every card states **what to do**, **why**, **supporting Open-Meteo data**, and **priority** across Morning, Outdoor, Cleaner Windows, Commute, Indoor, and Evening.

## 3. Indoor Air Advisor & Health-Guarded Eco Actions (45 seconds)
1. Open **Indoor Air Advisor** and show all 7 practical household topics calibrated to outdoor conditions—highlighting that ventilation guidance recommends short timed cross-ventilation during cleaner windows rather than keeping windows closed indefinitely.
2. Open **Eco Actions** and show how AIRWISE recommends public transit, carpooling, anti-idling, and errand batching without inventing fake CO₂ numbers, and triggers a safety override against outdoor walking/cycling whenever AQI reaches Unhealthy/Hazardous levels.

## 4. Ask AIRWISE & Transparency (45 seconds)
1. Open **Ask AIRWISE**, click an example prompt such as **"Is it safe to exercise outdoors now?"** or **"When should I travel today?"**, and show the structured, data-grounded answer and medical disclaimer.
2. Finish briefly on **About & Data Sources** to reinforce architectural clarity, API transparency, and honest limitations.
